Some single-color SVGs have an explicit brand-colored fill added for favicon use.

The remaining six brand files are locally created initial tiles: `amap`,
`doubao`, `feishu`, `ruanyifeng`, `sspai`, and `tongyi`.
These tiles identify the destination and do not reproduce its official logo.

`nas.svg`, `link.svg`, `../favicon.svg`, and the outline interface paths in
`../icons.js` were created locally for this site. The interface paths use a
simple, consistent 24×24 stroke style.

All default brand assets are served from `/icons/`; the site does not fetch
these assets from a third-party CDN at runtime. A custom site's explicitly
provided HTTP(S) favicon may be shown, with one attempt to fall back to
`/icons/link.svg` if it fails.

Public sites whose favicons could not be retrieved: Linux.do and 金山文档 (KDocs).
Their existing local fallback behavior is retained. Private-network destinations
were excluded from favicon discovery. `web/manifest.json` records the path, source
and detected format of every accepted image.

`kdocs.svg` is an original local K marker used when the KDocs favicon cannot be
retrieved. It is a fallback identifier, not a copy of the official KDocs logo.
